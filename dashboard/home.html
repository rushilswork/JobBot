How It Works
From zero to auto-applying
in under 10 minutes
1
Create account & build profile
Sign up (first user becomes admin). Go to Profile and fill in your details — or upload your resume and let AI extract everything: name, contact, skills, experience, education, salary expectations.
2
Configure filters & portals
Edit config/config.yaml to set your target keywords, locations, and which portals to enable. Optionally add a free Groq or Gemini API key in AI Settings to unlock match scoring and cover letters.
3
Start discovery & review
Hit Start Discovery in the dashboard. Jobs stream in filtered by your keywords. Each gets AI scored (with key on), skill-matched, and cover-letter pre-generated. Browse your inbox, approve what you like, skip the rest.
4
Auto Apply runs hands-free
Click Auto Apply on any reviewed job. JobBot opens the portal in a browser, fills every field from your profile — name, experience, skills, salary, cover letter — and submits. You get a confirmation with a screenshot. No typing, no tabs.
